# Reversal And Session Hypotheses

## Level Bounce

Idea: use daily highs/lows as support/resistance and trade intraday rejection/bounce.

Production parity rule:

- Buy only when candle low actually touches support and closes above it.
- Sell only when candle high actually touches resistance and closes below it.
- Do not repeat the same day/side/level entry.

Risk:

- Counter-trend entries can be dangerous when market breaks into a new trend.
- Needs a trend/volatility filter or small position sizing.

## Failed Breakout

Idea: fade a breakout if price pierces a recent daily level but closes back inside the range.

Entry:

- Short: high breaks resistance, close back below resistance.
- Long: low breaks support, close back above support.

Exit:

- ATR target or opposite side of the rejection candle.

Risk:

- On real trend days this is equivalent to shorting strength / buying weakness.

## Opening Range Breakout

Idea: define morning range after FORTS opens and trade continuation after range break.

Entry:

- Build range from the first 30-60 minutes after 10:00 MSK.
- Long on close above range high, short on close below range low.

Exit:

- Opposite side of range, measured move target, or session cutoff.

Risk:

- Needs enough liquidity at open.
- Energy contracts can already move before local open due to external markets.

## Volatility Regime Filter

Idea: do not trade all days the same way.

Rules to test:

- Breakout strategies only when ATR/range is expanding.
- Mean reversion only when daily range is normal and price is inside prior levels.
- Skip very thin candles and low-volume evening segments.
